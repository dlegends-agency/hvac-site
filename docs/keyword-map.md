## Notes on the sheet's cautions

- **Brand claims (HV-001 to HV-003):** the sheet says to confirm you are an authorized installer before claiming. We are a referral
  service, so the pages discuss brands neutrally and tell readers to ask contractors for proof of authorization.
- **Rebates (HV-020):** pages point to utilities, state energy offices, IRS guidance and DSIRE instead of stating amounts, because programs change.
- **After-hours (HV-018):** the page tells readers to ask about real availability. It makes no availability claims.
- **Financing (HV-019):** we do not lend or promise approval; the page lists what to check in terms.
- **[city] variants:** location pages list these service guides rather than duplicating each guide per city, to avoid thin doorway pages.
  Add genuinely local content (pricing, codes, case studies, photos, listings) before creating city-specific versions of a service page.
